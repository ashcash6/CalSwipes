## Residential dining halls

The four residential dining halls with full breakfast, lunch, and dinner menus in the feed are Crossroads, Cafe 3, Foothill, and Clark Kerr Campus.

## Retail and cafe locations

CalSwipes also knows the feed names for retail locations: Golden Bear Cafe, Bear Market, Cub Market, Local x Design, The Den, Qualcomm Cafe, and Gateway Cafe. Retail locations serve fixed portions, so CalSwipes recommends single items there instead of building combinations.
