## Which allergens are tracked

Each recipe in the Berkeley Dining menu feed carries a yes or no flag for twelve allergens: Milk, Egg, Fish, Shellfish, Tree Nuts, Wheat, Peanuts, Soybeans, Gluten, Alcohol, Sesame, and Pork. CalSwipes shows these flags exactly as the feed publishes them and never guesses a missing flag.

## Cross-contact and why nothing is guaranteed

Dining halls prepare many dishes in shared kitchens with shared equipment and shared serving lines. Even when an item's allergen flags all say no, cross-contact is possible. An allergen flag describes the recipe, not the plate in front of you. No item can be guaranteed to be free of an allergen.

## What to do if you have a serious allergy

If you have a severe or life-threatening allergy, talk to the dining hall manager or a dietitian before eating, ask staff about preparation for the specific item, and carry your prescribed medication. Do not rely on an app, a chatbot, or a menu flag alone.

## Recipes can change

Ingredients and suppliers can change without notice, so allergen flags can change from one day to the next. Check the flags for the specific date and meal you plan to eat.
